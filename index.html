1. Who We Are

Herbify is developed and operated by Daison Information Technology Solutions, a business registered with the Philippine Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) / Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

We are the Personal Information Controller responsible for your personal information under the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

Contact: herbify.apps@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to make Herbify work. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Email address — required to create and access your account
  • Display name (optional) — shown next to your community posts
  • Account password — stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt). We can never see or recover your plain-text password.
  • Community posts (testimonies) — herbal experiences, comments, ratings, and likes you create
  • Reports & helpfulness votes — feedback you submit on community content, used for moderation
  • In-app notifications — automated messages we create for you when something happens to your content (e.g., a temporary hold while we review reports). These are visible only to you, inside the app — we don't send notification emails or push notifications.
  • Saved herbs — herbs you bookmark in the app
  • Wellness journal entries (optional) — when you choose to log a daily entry, we store the date, the symptoms you selected from our list, and any free-text notes you add. These entries are private to your account and visible only to you. They are never shared with other users or third parties.
  • Symptom Checker selections — when you use the Symptom Checker feature to look up herbal options, your symptom selections for that lookup are processed only on your device and are not stored on our servers. (This is separate from Wellness journal entries above, which you explicitly save.)

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Unique user ID, sign-up date, last sign-in date — for account management
  • Authentication tokens — short-lived session tokens to keep you signed in (managed by Supabase)
  • Operational server logs — our database provider (Supabase) automatically records standard request metadata (IP address, timestamp, request type) for security and abuse prevention. These logs are retained for up to 7 days and are not used for analytics, profiling, or advertising.

2.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your real name, phone number, address, date of birth, or government IDs.
  • We do not track your location (no GPS, no IP-based location features).
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook, etc.), or social media pixels.
  • We do not collect biometric data or government-issued identification numbers.
  • We do not collect crash reports, telemetry, or behavioral analytics from the app at runtime.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on infrastructure provided by trusted sub-processors, listed in full in Section 12. Our primary storage is Supabase, which uses industry-standard security including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption at rest.

Supabase servers used by Herbify are located in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1 region). Transactional emails (e.g., password resets) are sent via Brevo, whose servers are in the European Union.

Cross-border transfer: By using Herbify, you consent to your data being transferred to and stored in these locations. We rely on our sub-processors' Standard Contractual Clauses and security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2, or equivalent) as the safeguard for such transfers, in line with NPC Circular 16-02 on cross-border data transfers.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in these limited situations:

  • With Supabase — to store and retrieve your account and content
  • With Brevo — to send transactional emails (password resets, account confirmation)
  • With other Herbify users — anything you post in the Community section is publicly visible
  • When legally required — in response to valid court orders, subpoenas, or lawful government requests under Philippine law
  • To protect safety — when necessary to protect life, prevent fraud, or prevent serious harm

In-app notifications are never shared with anyone other than the account holder they belong to. They are not visible to other users, not sent via email or push notification, and not shared with any third party.

6. Public Content

Anything you post in the Community section — testimonies, comments, ratings, and likes — is public within the app. Other users can see your display name, the herb you wrote about, your rating, your story, and your comments.

Please don't share sensitive info: Avoid posting full names, addresses, phone numbers, or specific medical diagnoses in community posts. Once posted, your content is visible to all Herbify users.

Your Wellness journal entries are NOT part of the Community section — they are private to your account and are never displayed publicly. The same applies to your in-app notifications — they are visible only to you.

You can delete your own posts at any time directly within the app. Posts that violate our community rules may be hidden or removed by our moderation system. When a post of yours is temporarily hidden by our moderation system, we will create an in-app notification to let you know — only you will see this notification.

7. Your Rights

Under the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012, as a data subject you have the following rights, which you can exercise free of charge:

  • Right to be informed — to know what data we collect and why (this policy serves that purpose)
  • Right to access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to object — refuse the processing of your data in certain cases
  • Right to rectification — fix inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to erasure or blocking — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to damages — be compensated for damages from inaccurate, incomplete, or unauthorized use of your data
  • Right to data portability — receive a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used electronic format
  • Right to file a complaint — lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your rights have been violated

How to exercise your rights

Email us at herbify.apps@gmail.com. We respond to verified requests within 30 days, in line with NPC guidelines. You can also delete your own community posts and comments directly within the app at any time.

Account deletion

You have two ways to permanently delete your account and all associated data:

  • Option 1 — In-app (immediate): Open Herbify, go to Profile → Delete Account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap the delete button. Your account and associated data (testimonies, comments, wellness journal entries, saved herbs, helpfulness votes, post reports, in-app notifications) are removed from our active database immediately. Backups are purged within 90 days.
  • Option 2 — Email request: Email us at herbify.apps@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process the deletion within 30 days.

Both methods remove the same data. The in-app option is faster and is confirmed by the app the moment you complete it.

8. Data Retention

  • Account data (email, hashed password) — kept while your account is active. Deleted immediately upon in-app account deletion, or within 30 days of an emailed deletion request.
  • Community posts (testimonies, comments) — kept until you delete them or your account. Hidden posts (due to moderation) are retained for up to 90 days for appeal review, then deleted.
  • Wellness journal entries — kept until you delete them individually or delete your account.
  • Reports & helpfulness votes — kept for 1 year, then anonymized.
  • In-app notifications — kept until you delete your account. We may purge notifications older than 1 year as part of routine database maintenance.
  • Operational server logs — retained by Supabase for up to 7 days for security and abuse prevention, then deleted.
  • Backups — purged within 90 days after account deletion.

9. Children's Privacy

Herbify is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it as quickly as possible.

Users aged 13–17 may use Herbify with the consent of a parent or guardian, in line with NPC advisory opinions on minors' consent.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at herbify.apps@gmail.com.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage at our backend provider
  • Password hashing with bcrypt (passwords are never stored in plain text)
  • Row-level security policies that prevent users from accessing each other's private data (including in-app notifications, which are only readable by the account they belong to)
  • Production credentials stored in encrypted environment variables, never in source code
  • No payment information is collected — Herbify is and always will be free

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If we ever discover a personal data breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours, as required by NPC Circular 16-03 on personal data breach management.

11. Health and Medical Information Disclaimer

Herbify provides general information about traditional Philippine herbal remedies and common over-the-counter medicines for educational purposes only. It is not a medical app, and the information it provides is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

The Symptom Checker feature is an educational reference tool that suggests possible herbal options for common, non-emergency complaints. It is not a substitute for professional medical evaluation.

⚠️ In an emergency, call 911 immediately or go to the nearest hospital. Herbify is not designed for emergency medical situations. For mental health crises in the Philippines, the NCMH Crisis Hotline is available 24/7 at 1553 (landline) or 1800-1888-1553 (toll-free mobile).

Information on Philippine common medicines is referenced from the Philippine National Formulary (8th Edition) published by the Department of Health, in compliance with Administrative Order 2015-0053. Herbify does not sell, dispense, or recommend specific products. All decisions about medication use must be made with a licensed doctor or pharmacist.

Any health-related information you choose to share in your community posts is voluntarily disclosed by you and becomes public. We treat your community posts as user-generated content, not as confidential health records. Your private Wellness journal entries are stored securely and are not made public.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before using any herbal remedy — especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, have a medical condition, or are giving herbs to children or the elderly.
