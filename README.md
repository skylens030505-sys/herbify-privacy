  <h2 id="what">2. What Information We Collect</h2>

  <h3>2.1 Information you provide directly</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Email address</strong> — required for account creation and sign-in.</li>
    <li><strong>Password</strong> — stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. We never see or store your raw password.</li>
    <li><strong>Testimonies and community posts</strong> — content you choose to publish in the in-app community, tied to your account.</li>
    <li><strong>Reports and helpfulness votes</strong> — feedback you submit on community content, used for moderation.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>2.2 Information collected automatically</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Anonymous crash reports</strong> — collected via Expo's built-in error reporting. These contain device model, OS version, and stack traces. They do NOT contain your email, name, location, or any post content.</li>
    <li><strong>Authentication tokens</strong> — short-lived session tokens managed by Supabase, used to keep you signed in.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>2.3 Information we do NOT collect</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>We do not collect your real name, phone number, address, date of birth, or government IDs.</li>
    <li>We do not track your location (no GPS, no IP-based location).</li>
    <li>We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.</li>
    <li>We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook, etc.).</li>
    <li>We do not collect health, biometric, or sensitive personal data as defined by Section 3(l) of the Data Privacy Act.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 id="store">5. How Long We Keep Your Data</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Account data (email, hashed password):</strong> kept while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.</li>
    <li><strong>Testimonies and community posts:</strong> kept until you delete them or your account. Hidden posts (moderation) are retained for up to 90 days for appeal review, then deleted.</li>
    <li><strong>Reports and votes:</strong> kept for 1 year, then anonymized.</li>
    <li><strong>Anonymous crash reports:</strong> kept by Expo for up to 90 days per their retention policy.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 id="security">6. How We Protect Your Data</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>All data is transmitted using HTTPS / TLS encryption.</li>
    <li>Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes — even we cannot read them.</li>
    <li>Database access is restricted with row-level security policies (Supabase RLS).</li>
    <li>Production credentials are stored in encrypted environment variables, never in source code.</li>
    <li>We do not store payment information of any kind. Herbify is and always will be free.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    No system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a personal data breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours, as required by NPC Circular 16-03.
  </p>

    <li><strong>Right to object</strong> — to refuse the processing of your data in certain cases.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to rectification</strong> — to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to erasure or blocking</strong> — to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").</li>
    <li><strong>Right to damages</strong> — to be compensated for damages from inaccurate, incomplete, or unauthorized use of your data.</li>
