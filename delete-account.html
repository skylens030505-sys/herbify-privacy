Delete Your Herbify Account

How to request deletion of your Herbify account and associated data.

Herbify is a Philippine herbal & wellness reference app operated by Daison Information Technology Solutions. If you have a Herbify account, you can delete it and its associated data at any time using either method below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Herbify app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and all associated data are permanently removed.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, send a deletion request to herbify.apps@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete My Account”. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

Data type What happens
Account info (email address, user ID) Permanently deleted
Wellness journal entries Permanently deleted
Community posts & testimonies you created Permanently deleted
Saved herbs & in-app preferences Permanently deleted
Records required for legal or fraud-prevention purposes May be retained only where required by law, then deleted

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
